The purchase is securely processed through Google Play In-App Billing. Upon completing the one-time payment of $39 Mexican Pesos (or approximately $2 USD in your local currency), the lifetime license is permanently bound to your Google Account. There are no recurring charges, renewals, or hidden fees. If you reinstall the app or upgrade your phone, your purchase restores automatically.
The free mode lets you scan up to 10 physical business cards to verify OCR accuracy and speed on your own device. It includes folder organization and instant global search. Once you reach 10 scans, you can upgrade to the lifetime license to scan unlimited cards.
Yes, 100%. Card scanning, optical character recognition (OCR), folder structuring, QR code generation, and contact exports work seamlessly in airplane mode or in venues without cell reception (such as convention center basements or flights).
Your Digital Card generates an industry-standard vCard 3.0 QR code. The other person only needs to point their standard iPhone or Android camera at your screen or saved image.
